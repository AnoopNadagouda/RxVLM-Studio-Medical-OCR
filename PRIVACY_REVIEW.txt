1. SCOPE & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This document establishes the clinical data privacy, ethics, and de-identification 
protocols implemented across the RxVLM Studio Medical OCR and Knowledge Graph 
repository. The objective is to ensure full compliance with healthcare privacy 
standards, including HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identification (45 CFR § 164.514(b)) and 
GDPR pseudonymization guidelines, prior to public repository hosting.

2. DE-IDENTIFICATION PROTOCOLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All medical prescription documents, manifests, annotations, and sample datasets 
included in this repository have undergone rigorous multi-layer anonymization:

a. Patient Identifiers:
   - All patient names (e.g., Rajesh Kumar, Ananya Sharma, etc.) are synthetic 
     pseudonyms randomly drawn from demographic name generator pools.
   - Ages, genders, and addresses are synthetically generated and do not map to 
     any real patient identity.

b. Attending Physicians & Institutions:
   - Prescribing physician names (e.g., Dr. S. K. Gupta, Dr. R. N. Mukherjee) and 
     department affiliations are standardized demo placeholders.
   - Hospital registration numbers, phone numbers, and institution letterheads 
     have been redacted or replaced.

c. Prescription Dates & Timestamps:
   - Calendar dates and session timestamps are calibrated synthetic test dates 
     designed to preserve temporal sequencing for dosage scheduling audits.

d. Image Source & Redaction:
   - Prescription handwriting images are sourced exclusively from public research 
     corpora with all direct personal identifiers masked/removed.

3. SENSITIVITY & COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Protected Health Information (PHI): 0 instances of unmasked real PHI detected.
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII): 0 instances of unmasked real PII detected.
- Clinical Safety Audits: Medication strength, dosage schedules, and drug names 
  are retained purely for scientific evaluation of PaddleOCR-VL model accuracy and 
  knowledge graph relational extraction.

4. USAGE & DISCLAIMER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The models, datasets, manifests, and software in this repository are published for 
academic research, benchmark reproducibility, and clinical NLP evaluation purposes 
only. They do not constitute automated medical advice or direct patient diagnosis.
